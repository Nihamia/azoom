Terms and Conditions

Please read these reservation terms before booking a car.

These terms explain how reservations and the rental process work on the AZoom website. Please read them before making a reservation.

1. Reservations and availability

Choose an available vehicle, pickup branch, and rental dates, then review the booking summary before confirming. A reservation is saved only after you confirm it. Vehicle availability may change before confirmation, and an unavailable car cannot be reserved.

The displayed rental amount is an estimate based on the listed sample daily rate and rental period, less any eligible rewards discount. Additional charges may be added after the return inspection.

2. Driver and booking details

Provide accurate contact and booking information. The person collecting and driving the car must hold a valid driving licence for the vehicle class and follow applicable Singapore road rules. Bring your booking receipt and licence when collecting the car.

3. Pickup and optional requests

Collect the car at the branch and location shown in your booking details. Check the vehicle and its included equipment before leaving, and tell staff about any visible issue. Child seats, pet-friendly arrangements, and accessibility requests are subject to availability and are not confirmed until staff approves them.

4. Use and return of the car

Use the car carefully and only for lawful purposes. Return it by the return date in your booking, following the return instructions and using a designated return point. Secure the car and place the key in the stated drop box where the return instructions require it.

The customer pickup checklist is a guide and does not replace the staff inspection. A condition not recorded on the checklist may still be reviewed during the return inspection. The customer may be responsible for damage that occurred during the rental, subject to staff review and the final inspection record.

5. Inspection, bills, and appeals

After return, staff may inspect the car and record comments, photographs, and repair costs. The final bill may include the rental charge and any repair charges recorded in the inspection. If you disagree with a bill, submit an appeal through your account so staff can review it. A revised bill, if issued, replaces the earlier amount shown for payment.

6. Cancellation and rewards

You may cancel a reservation from your account while it is still reserved. Once staff marks a car as rented out, contact the branch to discuss any change. In this prototype, City EV rentals earn 100 points, Family EV rentals earn 200 points, and Premium EV rentals earn 300 points after staff records the bill as received. A balance of 1,000 points can be used for a 10% discount on a later booking.
