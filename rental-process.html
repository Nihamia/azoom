STEP 02 - PICK UP

Collect your car at the branch.

An employee checks your booking and driver's licence, then shows you the vehicle.

  • Have your reference ready.
  • Bring your driver's licence.
  • Check the car with staff.
  • Ask about charging and returns.

BEFORE YOU DRIVE

Check the car together.

Make sure the car and branch match your reservation. Record existing marks before leaving.

STEP 03 - RETURN

Return to the designated car park.

Park in the assigned AZoom bay and follow the signs. An employee inspects the returned car.

  • Park in the assigned bay.
  • Note the return time and odometer.
  • Remove belongings and return the key.
